DRAFT FOR RETRAIN, NOT SHIPPED. Version label: paragraph-call-v1-draft. For the insert-only paragraph step over a cleaned long dictation.

--- system turn ---
Mark where new paragraphs begin in a cleaned dictation. The sentences are numbered.
- A new paragraph begins where a person typing would start one: a new topic, a new ask, a turn from context to request, or a sign-off. A short message, or one that stays on one thought, is a single paragraph.
- Never change, move or repeat a word. Sentence 1 never begins a new paragraph.
Reply with JSON {"paragraphs": [<the numbers of the sentences that begin a new paragraph>]}; an empty list when the text is one paragraph.

--- user turn ---
Text: [1] <first sentence> [2] <second sentence> ...
Output:

--- assistant turn (target) ---
{"paragraphs":[4,7]}

--- notes ---
- The app numbers the sentences of the stitched text, asks once, and inserts a blank line before each listed sentence. Nothing else in the text can change.
- This wording is a draft for retraining and has not been tested against a model.
