# Code of conduct

Hushy follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
That page is the full text and applies to every space the project runs, including issues, pull
requests and discussions.

In short, be respectful, assume good faith, give and accept review without making it personal, and
do not harass anyone. Maintainers may remove comments, close threads and block people who break
these rules.

To report a problem, write to hello@tryhushy.com. The maintainers read reports privately and will
not share the reporter's name without their consent.
