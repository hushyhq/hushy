Hushy word-frequency table (Sources/MurmurCore/Lexicon/WordFrequencyData.swift)

The table holds about 87,000 lowercase English word forms and a coarse frequency grade for each. It contains no
text from any document. The grades were computed by counting words in two shards (00000 and 00512 of 1024) of the
English part of the C4 dataset ("Colossal Clean Crawled Corpus", Raffel et al., 2020, "Exploring the Limits of
Transfer Learning with a Unified Text-to-Text Transformer", JMLR 21(140)), as released by the Allen Institute for AI
at https://huggingface.co/datasets/allenai/c4.

C4 is licensed under the Open Data Commons Attribution License (ODC-By) v1.0,
https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/by/1-0/ . Its text comes from the Common Crawl web corpus
(https://commoncrawl.org) and is subject to the Common Crawl terms of use.

This product includes frequency data derived from the C4 dataset, used under ODC-By 1.0.
